# Diffusion Models for Generating Two-Phase Morphologies in Organic Photovoltaic Solar Cells

This repository contains the final version of our research paper along with all supplementary materials, code, and figures. In our work, we explore the use of diffusion models to generate and optimize designs of two-phase morphologies suitable for organic photovoltaic (OPV) solar cells. The approach not only brings innovative design insights but also provides a framework for improving device performance.

## Paper Overview

In this paper, we detail how state-of-the-art diffusion models can be employed to:
- Generate realistic and novel morphologies for OPV materials.
- Analyze and compare the performance of generated designs with traditional approaches.
- Provide a comprehensive discussion on potential improvements in solar cell efficacy.

For the full details, please refer to our final published paper:
